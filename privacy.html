Privacy Policy

Three-Floor Chess · Last updated 12 August 2026

The short version

Three-Floor Chess is a game, not a data business. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and it never sells anything about you. Most of the game runs entirely on your device.

What the game stores, and where

  • Playing offline or against the computer: everything stays on your device. Settings and game data live in your browser's / app's local storage and never leave it.
  • Playing online with a friend: moves and chat messages travel directly between the two players (peer-to-peer, via WebRTC). A connection broker and, when needed, a relay server help the two devices find each other; they carry the connection but the game does not store your games on any server of ours.
  • Optional account (sign-in): if you create an account, your email address, chosen display name, friend list, and rating (Elo) are stored with our database provider (Supabase) solely to provide those features. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to us.

What the game does NOT do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking pixels.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Chat

In-game chat goes directly to your opponent over the peer-to-peer connection. It is not stored after the game ends.

Deleting your data

Offline data: clear the site/app data in your browser or uninstall the app. Account data: contact us at the address below and we will delete your account and everything attached to it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data unless an account is voluntarily created.
